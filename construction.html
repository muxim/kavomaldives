Why it matters

The programme problem

The difference

Our own supply line

Most construction delays in the Maldives are not construction problems. They are a container that did not sail, a clearance that sat, or a supplier who quoted a lead time they could not keep.

We buy, ship and clear the materials ourselves. When something slips we know in the same week, and we have stock in Hulhumalé to work around it. That is the whole argument for hiring a contractor who also imports.
