About Kavo

A Maldivian company that buys, builds and keeps.

Kavo Private Limited has traded from Male’ since 2009. We are 140 people across an office, a warehouse and live sites, and everything we do sits somewhere on one chain.

How it started

It began with other people’s cement

Kavo started in 2009 importing construction materials into Male’ — cement, steel and aggregate for contractors who were tired of a supply chain that made promises it could not keep. We were a trading company that happened to know a great deal about building programmes.

The contracting followed, because customers kept asking whether we could also put the material up. The property came after that, because the best answer to “is this a well-built building?” turned out to be owning it and letting it ourselves.

Why it holds together

One chain, not three companies

Plenty of groups list three businesses on a website and run them as strangers. Ours are the same chain read at different points: what we import is largely what we build with, and what we build is largely what we manage.

That has a practical consequence. When a shipment slips, the site team knows the same day. When a building has a defect at year three, the people who built it are still the people getting the call.

How we work

Three commitments

Say the real date

We quote dates we can keep

A programme built on optimistic lead times is a programme that fails in month four. We price the wait, put it in writing, and tell you early when something moves.

One contact

The person who quoted it runs it

No handover from sales to delivery. A weekly report, photographs, and a phone answered from the site rather than from an office in Male’.

Stay on the hook

We are still here at year five

We manage and maintain buildings we constructed. It is the strongest incentive there is to build them properly the first time.
